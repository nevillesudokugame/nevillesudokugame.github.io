Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026
Publisher: Vaidehi Mevada
Contact: nevillesudoku@gmail.com

Overview

Nimvelle Sudoku works offline without an account. Puzzle progress, notes, move history, settings, daily history, statistics, achievements, and cosmetic rewards are stored locally on your device. Version 1 does not operate a game backend or first-party analytics service.

The app does not request access to precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, SMS, or call logs.

Advertising

When consent, regional rules, and app configuration permit, Nimvelle Sudoku uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process IP address and coarse location inferred from it, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, fraud-prevention information, and consent choices to request, select, deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertisements.

Review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information.

Consent and tracking choices

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request current consent information and display a consent form where required. Eligible users can revisit choices through Settings → Privacy Choices. Rejecting consent or encountering a consent or network error does not block Sudoku.

The initial iOS configuration does not request App Tracking Transparency. If a future version introduces behavior requiring Apple-defined tracking, the app and this policy will be updated before that behavior is enabled. Core functionality remains available when advertising consent is rejected.

Local data and deletion

First-party game data remains in local app storage until you use Settings → Reset local data or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled. Advertising-data retention is controlled by Google and its partners under their policies.

Sharing and international processing

The publisher does not sell first-party Sudoku progress. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and authorized advertising partners as needed to provide and secure advertising, including in countries other than your own under their safeguards and legal bases.

Children

Nimvelle Sudoku is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. It is not offered in Apple’s Kids Category and is not marketed as a children’s app. If the intended audience changes, production advertising will remain disabled until a dedicated children’s-privacy and store-policy review is completed.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or appeal. Reset locally stored game data within the app or contact nevillesudoku@gmail.com for other inquiries. Requests involving Google-controlled data may need to be directed to Google.

Advertisement reports

Use Settings → Report an Advertisement or contact the support address to report inappropriate, misleading, or technically problematic advertising.

Security and changes

The app uses platform storage, encrypted transport supplied by advertising services, minimal permissions, and configuration safeguards. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. This policy may be updated when functionality, SDKs, law, or practices change; the effective date identifies the current version.
